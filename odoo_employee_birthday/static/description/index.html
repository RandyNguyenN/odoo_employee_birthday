Employee Birthday Tracker

A birthday dashboard for HR: who celebrates today, in 3, 7 and 15 days, a list of the next birthdays with a countdown and a month-by-month chart — straight from the Date of Birth already on your employees.

✉ Email us See all SunMesa Labs apps →
Free | No configuration · Only needs Employees · Community & Enterprise
😓The Problem
  • ✗ Birthdays are stored on each employee but standard Odoo has no view of who celebrates today or this week.
  • ✗ HR finds out about a birthday on the day itself — too late for a card, a cake or a message from the team.
  • ✗ Sorting employees by date of birth sorts by year, not by the next upcoming birthday.
  • ✗ Keeping a separate birthday spreadsheet means the list is always out of date.
🎯The Solution
  • ✓ Four KPI cards — Today, In 3 Days, In 7 Days and In 15 Days, counted from the Date of Birth.
  • ✓ Click a card to open the matching employees in a standard list.
  • ✓ Upcoming list — the next birthdays sorted by days remaining, with coloured "in Xd" badges.
  • ✓ Monthly chart — birthdays per month, with the current month highlighted.

Frequently Asked Questions

Where do the birthdays come from?
From the standard Date of Birth field on the employee (Private Information tab). Fill it in and the employee appears on the dashboard.
Is "In 7 Days" including "In 3 Days"?
Yes. The 3, 7 and 15 day cards each count every birthday from tomorrow up to that number of days, so a birthday in 2 days is in all three. Today's birthdays are only in the Today card.
Who can open the dashboard?
Odoo keeps the Date of Birth private to users with Employees access (Officer or Administrator), so the dashboard is meant for your HR team.
What about employees born on 29 February?
In years without a 29 February their birthday is counted on 28 February.
Does it send birthday e-mails?
No. The app is a dashboard and list for HR; it does not send messages or change employee data.
Does it need Odoo Enterprise?
No. It works on Community and Enterprise and only depends on the Employees app.
